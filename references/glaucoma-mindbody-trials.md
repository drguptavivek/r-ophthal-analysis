# Mind-Body Intervention Trials in Glaucoma

Load this for mindfulness-based stress reduction (MBSR), mindfulness meditation, yoga or pranayama trials in glaucoma or ocular hypertension. IOP analysis follows `glaucoma-endpoints.md`; trial design follows `clinical-study-designs.md`.

IOP is the primary endpoint, analysed as in a drug trial; stress, perfusion and quality-of-life measures are secondary.

| Endpoint group | Examples used in AIIMS trials (Dada et al. 2018-2022) | Analysis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IOP (primary) | Mean IOP at prespecified visits; change from baseline | Baseline-adjusted mixed model; both eyes clustered |
| Stress biomarkers | Serum cortisol, beta-endorphin, IL-6, TNF-alpha, ROS, total antioxidant capacity, BDNF | Log-transform skewed markers; report geometric mean ratios |
| Gene expression | Blood or trabecular meshwork panels | Prespecified genes; false discovery rate for panels |
| Ocular perfusion | OCTA optic disc vessel density | Eye-level, clustered |
| Quality of life | Glaucoma-specific or generic instruments | Validated scoring; state the minimal important difference |
| Adherence | Sessions attended, home-practice logs | By arm; prespecified per-protocol threshold |

This endpoint list comes from the trials' abstracts and keywords; confirm exact assays, sample types and time points in the full papers.

## Design and Analysis Rules

- Participants cannot be masked: mask the IOP assessor and record it; use an attention-matched or active control where possible.
- Keep IOP-lowering medication fixed, or record every change and handle it as an intercurrent event.
- Prespecify the primary time point and whether post-practice follow-up tests persistence.
- Intention to treat is primary; adherence-based analyses are secondary and labelled.
- Name one primary endpoint; treat biomarkers as exploratory or adjust for multiplicity.
- Report the intervention per TIDieR: content, dose, frequency, instructor qualification, fidelity checks.
